How Scan & Go works

1
Hold items up to the scanner

Place each product in front of the detection camera so the AI can recognize it — watch the Detection Monitor for confirmation.

2
Watch them appear in your cart

Recognized items are added automatically. Adjust quantities or remove lines from the cart if needed.

3
Pay with cash or card

When you’re ready, tap Checkout. Guests can use Cash; card pay needs Continue with a real email (live Paystack).

4
Need help?

Use Emergency anytime to report a problem. History shows your session purchases, and Settings adjusts display preferences.
